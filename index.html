About Me

From Developer to Deal-Maker

I started my career as a Salesforce Technical Analyst at TCS in 2016 — writing code, managing incidents, and figuring out how Salesforce actually works under the hood. What I discovered along the way was that my real strength wasn't in building the product. It was in explaining it, positioning it, and helping clients see its value.

That realisation led me to Presales — and I never looked back. Over the next several years, I worked across TCS, Brillio, and LTIMindtree, shaping Salesforce solutions for clients across Finance, Automobile, Realty, Hospitality, and Retail sectors, spanning EMEA, APAC, and the US.

Today, I work at Salesforce Professional Services as an Associate Engagement Manager, helping grow the Data and Marketing Cloud business across India — collaborating with C-suite stakeholders, building winning proposals, and connecting the right solutions to the right problems.

I'm also an MBA in Marketing & Analytics from Great Lakes Institute of Management, which means I approach every Salesforce conversation with both technical grounding and a commercial lens.

🌍 The TCS Middle East Milestone

During my time at TCS, I contributed to expanding the Salesforce practice into the EMEA region — collaborating with cross-domain teams to drive growth and helping establish TCS's Salesforce presence in new geographies.

📈 Growing PS India's Pipeline

At Salesforce Professional Services, I single-handedly managed Data and Marketing Cloud deals and helped acquire three new logos for PS India — from discovery to commercial close.

🎓 Research & Curiosity

My MBA thesis explored the effect of nostalgia on consumer buying behaviour — an early signal of my fascination with what actually drives decisions. That thinking still shapes how I approach client conversations today.
